I build statistical and research tools that make evidence synthesis, biostatistics, and scientific publishing more reproducible, and browser tools that let people see how the science works.

My work sits mostly at the intersection of **R**, **Python**, **Bayesian modeling**, **meta-analysis**, **research integrity**, **interactive visualization**, and **GNU/Linux**.

## Current focus

- Population-adjusted indirect treatment comparisons and target trial emulation methods
- Meta-research and research integrity: retractions, citation validation, transparency, FAIR metadata
- Interactive, backend-free browser tools for teaching anatomy, statistics, and mathematics
- Research with language models where every claim is checked against its primary source before it is published
- R packages for biostatistics workflows and reproducible research on GNU/Linux

## How I think about software

Good research software should be:

- **Reproducible** enough for another analyst to rerun it.
- **Transparent** enough for a reviewer to audit it.
- **Documented** enough for a tired user to understand it.
- **Small and boring** where possible; cleverness is not a substitute for reliability.
